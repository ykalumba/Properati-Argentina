## Requirements

* **Python 3**: Core execution environment.
* **Pandas**: Data manipulation, cleaning, and tabular data structuring.
* **NumPy**: Numerical operations and array handling.
* **SciPy**: Statistical testing and mathematical functions.
* **Scikit-learn**: Machine learning, model evaluation, and feature transformations.
* **Category Encoders**: Categorical feature encoding.
* **Seaborn**: Statistical data visualization.
* **Matplotlib**: Plot customization and static visualizations.
* **Plotly**: Interactive charts and exploratory data visualization.
* **Power BI**: Geographic mapping, interactive dashboards, and final reporting.
